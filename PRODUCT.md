# SHODHA AI
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product purpose
A local investigator can submit related image, video and speech files, then trace an assessment to the detectors and samples that produced it. The first screen prioritizes starting an investigation and reviewing recent reports.
## Operating context
The Windows FastAPI service accepts one active analysis per worker and retains up to 100 reports. Reports persist independently of source media. Outcomes describe uncalibrated model findings and may conflict.
## Stack and identity
React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Recharts and Lucide. A restrained charcoal and navy forensic workspace uses blue actions and cyan accents. The user prefers a working interface first.
